02 // Data Protection Protocol

Privacy Policy & Confidentiality

1. Absolute Discretion & Legal Framework

Discretion is the cornerstone of Vector Risk Exclusive (VRX). Vector Risk Management K.K. processes all client, principal, and corporate information in strict adherence to Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (個人情報保護法 — APPI) and international executive privacy standards.

2. Scope of Collected Information

Information submitted via our confidential consultation channels (principal name, corporate entity, secure email, venue parameters, and operational requirements) is used exclusively to evaluate threat assessments, prepare security proposals, and execute mandated protection operations.

3. Zero Third-Party Disclosure

VRX never sells, rents, or discloses client identities, itineraries, asset locations, or operational records to third parties, except where strictly required by Japanese law enforcement authorities under valid legal mandate.

4. Data Access & Erasure Requests

Principals may request immediate access, rectification, or permanent erasure of their operational records by contacting our Data Protection Officer at:
